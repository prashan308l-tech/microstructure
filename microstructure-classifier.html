Microstructure Classifier

Upload a light-optical micrograph of steel and the model predicts its family. It runs entirely in your browser; images are not uploaded anywhere.

Click to choose an image or drag and drop it here
JPG or PNG, ideally a 4:3 micrograph

About this model. Trained on 90 micrographs (30 each) of austenitic stainless, carbon and duplex stainless steel from the CRTI Algeria open dataset. Features: grey-level, colour, gradient and local-texture statistics over 128×128 patches, combined by logistic regression. Cross-validated image accuracy (held-out images): . It can only choose among these three families, so images of other materials, etchants or microscopes may be classified wrongly. Treat results as indicative, not as a lab verdict.
